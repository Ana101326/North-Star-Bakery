Our Bakery Story

North Star Bakery started with a simple idea: create a friendly neighborhood place where people can stop for fresh baked goods. We want to welcome families, people heading to work, and anyone planning a special event.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good baking starts with good ingredients. We plan to use fresh ingredients and make our baked goods in smaller batches so customers can enjoy them at their best.

Meet Our Team

Our bakers work on breads and pastries each morning, and our front counter team helps customers decide what to order. Everyone has a part in making the bakery feel welcoming.
